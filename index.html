Simple Past Tense

¡Aprende de forma súper fácil y divertida a hablar de acciones que empezaron y terminaron en el pasado! Domina verbos regulares (-ed), irregulares, pronunciación y expresiones de tiempo.

SELECCIONA UN BOTON

1. Estructuras del Simple Past Tense

Usamos el Simple Past para acciones que ocurrieron y terminaron en un momento específico del pasado.

🌸 Afirmativo (+)

Subject + Verb (Past) + Complement
  • We watched TV last night. (Regular)
  • I ate a delicious cake at home. (Irregular)
  • She traveled to Japan last year.

🧁 Negativo (-)

Subject + didn't + Verb (Base)

¡Ojo! Al usar didn't, el verbo vuelve a su forma base (sin -ed).

  • I didn't work at night.
  • They didn't go to the beach last week.

🎀 Interrogativo (?)

Did / Didn't + Subject + Verb (Base)?

Al preguntar con Did, también olvidamos el sufijo del pasado.

  • Did you work at night?
  • Did we buy food today?
⏰ Time Expressions (Expresiones de Tiempo):
Yesterday Last night Last week Last Saturday Last summer Last year Two days ago Five years ago In 1996

2. Reglas de Escritura (Spelling Rules - Regular Verbs)

Cómo agregar correctamente la terminación -ed a los verbos regulares.

Regla General: + ed

La mayoría de verbos solo añaden -ed.

Walk → Walked • Work → Worked • Clean → Cleaned
Terminados en -e: + d

Si el verbo ya termina en -e, solo agrega -d.

Live → Lived • Close → Closed • Dance → Danced
Consonante + y: -ied

Cambia la y por i y agrega -ed.

Study → Studied • Cry → Cried • Carry → Carried
Vocal + y: + ed

Si hay vocal antes de la y, NO se cambia.

Play → Played • Stay → Stayed
Consonante + Vocal + Consonante (CVC): Duplica

Si termina en 1 vocal + 1 consonante acentuada (excepto w, y), duplica la última consonante + -ed.

Stop → Stopped • Wrap → Wrapped • Commit → Committed • Prefer → Preferred

Pronunciación de la terminación "-ed"

Aunque todos terminan en "-ed" al escribirse, ¡tienen 3 sonidos diferentes! Haz clic en los botones para escucharlos.

/ t /

Cuando el verbo termina en sonido sordo: p, k, f, s, ch, sh, x, th

/ d /

Cuando el verbo termina en sonido sonoro: b, g, j, m, n, l, r, th, w, v, z

/ ɪd /

Cuando la última consonante del verbo es: t o d

Reading Comprehension: Daniel's Web Project 💻

Lee la historia de Daniel sobre diseño web y programación, y responde las 7 preguntas interactivas.

Last year, Daniel started a new career in web design and programming. He studied HTML, CSS, and JavaScript at a technical institute. Every day, he practiced how to create websites and designed simple web pages.

After a few months, Daniel created his first professional website for a small company. He developed the homepage, added images, and organized the content. He also tested the website on different devices and fixed several programming errors.

Daniel worked with a team of designers and programmers. They discussed the project, shared ideas, and solved technical problems together. Sometimes, Daniel used programming tools to improve the website’s performance.

At the end of the project, the client visited the website and liked the design. Daniel felt very happy because he learned many new skills. The experience helped him understand that web design and programming were important for his professional career.
